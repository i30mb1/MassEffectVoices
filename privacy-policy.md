# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

This policy describes how the Android app **Mass Effect Voices** (package `n7.mev`) handles your data.

## Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. It does not collect, store or share any personal or device data.

## Permissions

- **Modify system settings** (`WRITE_SETTINGS`) — only to set a voice line as your ringtone or notification sound when you ask for it.
- **Modify audio settings** (`MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS`) — to play voice lines.
- **Billing** (`com.android.vending.BILLING`) — to offer optional "support the developer" purchases.

Sound files you download are saved on your device only.

## Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details. Google's handling of that data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Hero modules

Heroes are downloaded on demand through Google Play (Play Feature Delivery). No data beyond what Google Play itself handles is involved.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
